#### Our first product: The Trade Analyser (Free to Use)

The **Trade Analyser**, is now live at our website. It’s a powerful, free tool designed to help finance and treasury teams get fast, actionable insights into their hedging activity.

With the Trade Analyser, you can:

<strong>- Uncover the hidden costs of hedging</strong> by seeing what counterparties earn on each trade

<strong>- Assess the potential impact of FX movements</strong> on your bottom line

<strong>- Evaluate the cost-benefit of your hedging strategy</strong> using real-time and historical data

It’s simple, powerful, and built to shine a light on areas that have been hidden for too long.<br><br>‍
