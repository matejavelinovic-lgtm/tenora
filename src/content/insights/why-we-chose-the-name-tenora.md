When we set out to name our company, we knew it needed to stand for more than just a product. It had to represent a shift in mindset—away from opaque systems and toward a future where transparency and better financial outcomes are the expectation, not the exception.

‍

#### A name inspired by purpose

Our name is inspired by the financial term **“tenor”**, which denotes the time remaining until a financial contract matures. It’s a technical word, used in FX and derivatives markets, but one that reflects the seriousness and precision of financial risk management. We adapted it to **Tenora**—a name that captures our **forward-thinking** approach to helping finance teams manage FX risk with **clarity**, **confidence**, and **data-driven insight**. It’s a nod to the professionals we serve, and a signal of the values we stand for.

‍

#### What Tenora stands for

In a space where pricing can be obscure, markups hidden, and outcomes unpredictable, we believe finance professionals deserve better. Tenora stands for:

‍

**Transparency**: Giving teams full visibility into what they’re being charged—and why.

**Fairness**: Eliminating hidden costs and letting data lead the decision.

**Clarity**: Simplifying the complex, so you can act with confidence.

**Better outcomes**: Because reducing risk shouldn’t come with added cost or confusion.

‍

#### A Name with Purpose

Tenora isn’t just a fintech brand. It’s a statement of purpose:

\- That finance leaders should be empowered.

\- That opacity has no place in modern FX management.

\- That smart, strategic decision-making should be supported by facts—not buried in fine print.

‍

We’re here to build that future, and we’re starting with the [Trade Analyser](/trade-analyser-lite).
