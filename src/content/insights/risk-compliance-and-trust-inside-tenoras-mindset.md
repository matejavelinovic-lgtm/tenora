In this interview with ComplyAdvantage, Tenora’s Chief Risk and Compliance Officer, Rebecca Robinson, shares how we approach risk and compliance as a strategic enabler, not a box-ticking exercise.

Rebecca discusses how embedding compliance into product design and decision-making helps finance teams navigate volatility with greater confidence, transparency, and control. From regulatory expectations to real-world risk management, the conversation explores why strong compliance foundations are essential for building resilient fintech infrastructure.
