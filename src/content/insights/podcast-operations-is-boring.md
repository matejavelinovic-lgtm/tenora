‍<strong>Episode title:</strong> <em>Operations is boring</em><br><strong>Speakers:</strong> Sophie Paterson (COO, Tenora) in conversation with Simon Tweddle, Managing Director at Shapes First<strong><br><br>Listen now:</strong> <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XA04cYM6cQw" target="_blank"><strong>Watch the full episode on YouTube</strong></a>

In this open, practical chat, Tenora’s COO Sophie Paterson talks with Simon about what it really takes to operationalise a fintech from early build to scale. She contrasts life in a PLC with the “three-hats” reality of a startup, where decisions touch cash flow, revenue, risk and culture—and where progress often means choosing what *not* to ship in year one. Sophie shares how a data-led approach to risk brings clarity to tough calls, why capturing decisions and actions matters, and how to build efficiency and resilience into products from day one rather than bolting them on later.

‍

**You’ll hear about:**

- Sensible prioritisation in year one - saying “not yet” without losing momentum.
- Viewing cash through ROI *and* risk lenses to support sustainable growth.
- Laying scalable foundations early.
- Creating the conditions for healthy challenge between ops, product and risk.
