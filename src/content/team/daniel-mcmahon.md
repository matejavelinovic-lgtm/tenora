Daniel is a Director in Macquarie’s Corporate FX and Rates Sales Team and brings 15 years of experience in foreign exchange and interest rates. He is responsible for overseeing the delivery of innovative solutions and strategic guidance to money service businesses and corporate treasury clients across EMEA. Before joining Macquarie in 2018, Daniel spent seven years at a leading FX fintech, focusing on the UK and Ireland. He holds a BA (Hons) in Economics from University College Dublin.
