Ed is a Chartered Accountant with 15 years of finance leadership experience. He trained at PwC in New Zealand and the UK before spending the last decade leading Corporate Finance at a real estate investment manager, overseeing finance operations and group reporting across multiple international entities. Ed has led financial modelling and due diligence for equity and debt transactions, and served on key governance committees. His combination of Big Four training and operational finance leadership brings both technical rigour and practical commercial insight to Tenora.
