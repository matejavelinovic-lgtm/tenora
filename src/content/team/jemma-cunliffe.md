Jemma has 20 years of experience across brand, product and marketing. Trained as an architect, she began her career leading communications and global events before co-founding OpenBlend, where she spent six years building the product and leading client success. She has since consulted on brand and marketing strategy for organisations across finance, digital publishing and philanthropy. Her mix of design, product and marketing experience gives her a practical understanding of how complex propositions are built, positioned and sold.
