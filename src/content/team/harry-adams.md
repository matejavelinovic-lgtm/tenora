With over two decades of leadership in the financial sector, Harry has played a pivotal role in transforming the B2B FX risk management and payments industry. He held senior roles at Travelex and Monex Europe, building deep expertise in foreign exchange. In 2012, he founded Argentex Group Plc, leading the business from startup to a successful IPO in 2019. Under his leadership, Argentex expanded internationally and secured regulatory licences in Dubai, the Netherlands, and Australia. Harry remained Group CEO until 2023, solidifying his reputation as a visionary in the industry.
