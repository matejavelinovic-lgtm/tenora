Sean is CEO of Financial Services at Fexco Group, a role he has held since 2022. Prior to Fexco, he spent his career in senior leadership positions across financial markets, investment management and insurance. This included serving as Group Treasurer and CEO of the Markets and Treasury Division at Bank of Ireland, where he also sat on the Group Executive Committee from 2014 to 2020. He has held board positions with a number of financial services firms, including New Ireland Assurance.
